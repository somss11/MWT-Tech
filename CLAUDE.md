## Company facts — read on demand

Anything about Mawkish itself (services, partners, offices, leadership, numbers, case studies,
messaging) comes from the November 2026 company profile deck. Before writing or editing any
company-facing copy, read the distilled summary `docs/company-profile/company-profile.md` — it is
not loaded automatically, to keep context small. Its USAGE RULES section governs what may be
published on the website. These rules always apply, even without opening it:

1. Case-study result percentages, module lists and integrations are **indicative, not verified** —
   never publish them without the user's explicit confirmation. The `99.7%` uptime figure on
   seven case studies is SAP's standard SLA, not a measured client result.
2. Vendor/partner facts (SAP, Salesforce, Databricks, MiHCM, Samishti, Deloitte, Odoo) must be
   re-checked on the vendor's official pages before going on the website.
3. **No Databricks track record** — position Databricks as capability and access only.
4. **No formal Anthropic partnership claim** — never say partner, reseller or certified.
5. Client names and logos are not automatically publishable; John Keells Restaurants' brand is
   not named, and the **Sun Siyam** logo/name must not be used until the user explains it.
6. The removed CIC Holdings pull-quote (Dhammika Jayasundara) must **never be reinstated or
   attributed** anywhere.
